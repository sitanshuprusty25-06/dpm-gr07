# Fine-print review — 12 November 2026

Scope: ten supported products, one domestic non-EMI purchase. No change to Gemini. This is a desk review of issuer documents, not bank confirmation or actual settled transaction testing.

- SBI: [FAQ](https://www.sbicard.com/en/faq/cashback-sbi-card-faq.page) and [April 2026 change](https://www.sbicard.com/sbi-card-en/assets/docs/pdf/cashback-revised.pdf). Separate online/offline caps retained. Actual merchant MCC and posting date control rewards. An explicitly unconfirmed route no longer produces a positive recommendation.
- Axis: [current cashback terms](https://www.axis.bank.in/docs/default-source/default-document-library/cashback-tncs---final.pdf), sections on capping and surplus, [issuer worked illustration](https://www.axis.bank.in/docs/default-source/default-document-library/terms-and-conditions-flipkart-axis-bank-credit-card.pdf), [preferred list](https://www.axis.bank.in/docs/default-source/default-document-library/flipkart-axis-bank-credit-card-preferred-merchants.pdf). After cap, immediate reward is limited to remaining allowance; surplus is only for adjustments. Food delivery on Swiggy is distinct from Instamart and Dineout. Flipkart Health not modeled as eligible Flipkart shopping. The selection labels require eligible transactions. 
- HDFC: ORNGE, original and BLCK product terms linked in catalogue. ORNGE section 2.3 names Amazon, Flipkart and Cleartrip; minimum ₹249 on 5% and ₹100 on base; original May 2026 section 2.3 expressly includes Amazon/Flipkart MCC 5411 and Cleartrip from April 17. BLCK table 2.1 names Myntra; do not infer every variant shares its list. Generic marketplace is not sufficient evidence. For original/BLCK Cleartrip special offer duration/cap not inferred; use only confirmed ordinary listed tier. HDFC rounding is an existing estimate; no claim of issuer-exact settlement rounding. Returns, reversals, business spending and statement posting affect actual rewards; not tracked.
- Amazon Pay ICICI: [partnership update](https://www.icici.bank.in/about-us/news-room/2025/amazon-pay-and-icici-bank-renew-partnership-enhance-indias-most-adopted-co-branded-credit-card) and [FAQ](https://www.icici.bank.in/personal-banking/cards/credit-card/amazon-pay-credit-card/faqs). Prime/non-Prime eligible Amazon Pay hotel travel 5/3%; eligible digital and bill payments 2%; ordinary external utilities excluded per newer partnership update. Gift cards/wallet loads intentionally out of scope despite eligible Amazon-specific exceptions. No claim all Amazon transactions earn the same rate.
- HSBC: [current page](https://www.hsbc.co.in/credit-cards/products/live-plus/) and [guide](https://www.hsbc.co.in/content/dam/hsbc/in/documents/credit-cards/live-plus-credit-card-services-guide.pdf). Current page supports ₹1,200 shared cap; older service-guide illustration has different ₹1,000 terms, so retain explicit uncertainty around unconfirmed MCC/post-cap cases instead of inventing a fallback. Amazon/Flipkart/Myntra 1.5% remains.
- Kiwi (refreshed 13 November 2026): [application page](https://apply.gokiwi.in/landing), [YES BANK product page](https://www.gokiwi.in/cards/yesbank/) and [rewards policy](https://gokiwi.in/rewards-policy-v2/) support 1.5% eligible QR, 0.5% eligible online, completed ₹100 blocks and no joining/annual card fee. The [cap FAQ](https://gokiwi.in/helpcenter/rewards/6a6/) illustrates a rupee cap of 1% of credit limit, while the policy refers to Kiwis; retain actual remaining rupee allowance as input, not an inferred conversion. Redemption requires 500-Kiwi blocks. [Neon](https://gokiwi.in/neon/neon2/) and [Boost](https://gokiwi.in/kiwi-boost-membership/) are paid optional plans, disclosed but outside the standard comparison along with issuer points, EMI and other partner cards. Excluded MCCs remain conditional.
- Scapia: the newer Federal agreement (October 2026 filename, indexed evidence only) confirms Visa ₹20 minimum and 10% coins, RuPay 5% above ₹500, eligible Scapia app travel 20% coins; Scapia product material (not reliably retrievable during link review) says ₹500 or more. Keep exactly ₹500 unresolved. Full new agreement was not retrievable for visual inspection; indexed issuer text is available. The catalogue links to the retrievable older agreement and Federal Bank product page; the newer agreement is retained as an evidence limitation, not a customer-facing link. Coins are redemption value, not cash. Rounding of RuPay points is not independently confirmed.

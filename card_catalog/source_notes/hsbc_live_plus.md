# HSBC Live+ (India) — evidence note

Source reviewed: 2026-11-12. Issuer:
- https://www.hsbc.bank.in/credit-cards/products/live-plus/
- https://www.hsbc.co.in/content/dam/hsbc/in/documents/credit-cards/live-plus-credit-card-services-guide.pdf

HSBC's current product material states 10% accelerated cashback on eligible dining, food delivery, grocery, shopping and utility spends **sharing a ₹1,200 cap per billing cycle**, and 1.5% on most other eligible domestic spends. The India service guide identifies eligible and excluded merchant-category codes (MCCs). Shopping at Amazon, Flipkart and Myntra earns 1.5%, not 10%. Some utility MCCs earn 0%; the general “utility bills” label is not enough to determine eligibility.

The available wording does not clearly establish whether a boosted-category transaction amount *above* the remaining accelerated cashback cap then earns the 1.5% base rate. The app therefore **withholds a definitive comparison** when that ambiguity would affect a ranking. It also withholds a shopping/utility ranking where merchant MCC is not sufficiently established.

The optional user input is cashback already earned from **all accelerated categories combined** in the current billing cycle, not only the selected category. No user history is stored.

Sources reflect publicly visible issuer material, not independently validated merchant MCCs. The Group 07 team should reconfirm before assessed submission.

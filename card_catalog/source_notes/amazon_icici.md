Evidence review: 2026-11-12. This note describes the current MVP model, not a fresh issuer confirmation.

## Modeled treatment and limitations
- Eligible Amazon India goods and modeled eligible Amazon Pay hotel travel: 5% Prime / 3% non-Prime.
- Eligible Login and Pay with Amazon partner checkout, digital purchases and qualifying Amazon Pay bill payments: 2%.
- Other eligible purchases: 1%; ordinary external utility payments and fuel earn no modeled reward. Combined Amazon/Flipkart grocery classification is unresolved for this card.
- Rewards are Amazon Pay balance, not statement credit. No modeled cashback cap; annual fee ₹0. Gift cards, wallet loads and promotional discounts are out of MVP scope.

# Flipkart Axis Bank

Product: https://cug.axisbank.com/cards/credit-card/flipkart-axisbank-credit-card

Cashback terms: https://www.axisbank.com/docs/default-source/default-document-library/cashback-tncs---final.pdf

August 2026 revision: https://campaign.axis.bank.in/generic/terms-and-conditions-generic-all.pdf

Myntra eligible purchases: 7.5%; Flipkart and Cleartrip: 5%; separate ₹4,000 cashback cap per merchant per statement quarter. Preferred merchants: 4%, uncapped; ordinary eligible purchases: 1%, uncapped. Accelerated cashback depends on merchant IDs. The app does not infer a preferred merchant or award base cashback after the accelerated cap; it withholds the winner if post-cap treatment is unresolved. No coupons, fees, or limited-time offers. Standard annual fee ₹500 plus tax. Source review: 12 November 2026; team to independently verify.

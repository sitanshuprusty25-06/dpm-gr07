# CASHBACK SBI Card — evidence note

Source reviewed: 2026-11-12. Issuer:
- https://www.sbicard.com/en/faq/cashback-sbi-card-faq.page
- https://www.sbicard.com/en/personal/credit-cards/cashback-sbi-card.html
- https://www.sbicard.com/sbi-card-en/assets/docs/pdf/cashback-revised.pdf

Effective 1 April 2026, the issuer specifies 5% for eligible online and 1% for eligible offline merchant POS transactions, with separate ₹2,000 cashback caps per statement cycle (maximum ₹4,000 in aggregate). Once a channel's cap is exhausted, later transactions in that channel earn no cashback in that statement cycle.

Exclusions include utility, insurance, fuel, rent, wallets, education, jewellery, railways, gaming, tolls, government transactions and merchant/Flexipay EMI (see full issuer terms). The app's category picker cannot identify every MCC. It gives a **conditional estimate**, not a guarantee.

The optional “cashback already earned” input is the cashback already credited/earned in the **relevant channel** during the current statement cycle, not previous transaction spend.

Evidence review: 2026-11-12. This note describes the current MVP model, not a fresh issuer confirmation.

## Modeled treatment and limitations
- Federal Visa: 10 coins per ₹100 of eligible domestic spend (2% estimated redemption value), ₹20 minimum; model rounds Visa coins to the nearest whole coin.
- Federal RuPay: 5 coins per ₹100 (1% estimated redemption value). Product material states ₹500 or more; agreement says above ₹500, so exactly ₹500 is unresolved. Proportional coin estimate is not verified settlement rounding.
- Eligible Scapia app travel: 20 coins per ₹100 (4% redemption value). Five coins equal ₹1 of Scapia redemption, not cash. Both cards have ₹0 listed annual fee.
- The newer agreement dated 23 October 2026 was indexed but not fully retrievable for visual inspection. Older agreement and product references remain listed; verify current eligibility before relying on estimates.
- Fuel, utility and other excluded MCCs apply. No forex, airport perks, lounge, non-transaction benefits or redemption fees modeled. Select the exact Federal Bank network, not another issuer.

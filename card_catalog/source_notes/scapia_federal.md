# Scapia Federal Visa and RuPay

Sources: https://www.federal.bank.in/scapia
https://www.federal.bank.in/documents/d/guest/cardholder_agreement-v11-30_04_26

Visa 10 coins per ₹100 eligible domestic spend, minimum ₹20; RuPay 5 coins per ₹100 eligible spends. Product material states ₹500 or more, agreement uses more than ₹500; exact ₹500 unresolved. Eligible Scapia travel: 20 coins per ₹100, subject to issuer eligibility. Five coins = ₹1 of Scapia redemption value. No cash equivalence guarantee. Round coins to nearest whole coin as illustrated for Visa; RuPay estimates use proportional coin value. No airport, forex or non-transaction perks modeled. Excluded categories in agreement.
